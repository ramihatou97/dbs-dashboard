About This Dashboard

This dashboard visualizes geographic and socioeconomic barriers to Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) surgery access across Canada. Data includes 920 verified distance records, corrected coordinates for remote Arctic communities, and comprehensive vulnerability assessments. All maps are fully interactive - zoom, pan, and click markers for detailed information.

Map 1: Travel Burden Heatmap

Visualizes distance-based travel burden across Canada. Critical FSAs (red) require >500km travel to nearest DBS center.

Map 2: Vulnerability Index

Combines distance with socioeconomic factors (income, employment, housing) to identify high-vulnerability regions.

Map 3: Indigenous Access Crisis

Highlights disproportionate barriers faced by Indigenous communities, with 40 FSAs showing >5% Indigenous population and extreme distances.

Map 4: Multi-Barrier Service Gaps

Identifies FSAs facing multiple simultaneous barriers (distance + low income + high Indigenous population).

Map 5: Patient Flow Lines

Shows patient flow patterns from all 511 FSAs to their nearest DBS centers, with dramatic directional arrows indicating travel routes.
