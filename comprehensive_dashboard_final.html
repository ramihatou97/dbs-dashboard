Executive Summary

This comprehensive dashboard presents eight specialized interactive visualizations analyzing geographic and socioeconomic disparities in access to Deep Brain Stimulation (DBS) therapy across Canada. The analysis encompasses 512 Forward Sortation Areas (FSAs) and 11 DBS treatment centers, revealing significant barriers to equitable healthcare access. Enhanced with dramatic patient flow visualizations, directional arrows, Sankey diagrams, and multi-dimensional vulnerability assessments.

Interactive Visualizations

🗺️ Map 1: Travel Burden Heatmap

Five-tier geographic visualization revealing travel distance disparities from excellent access zones (<50km in green) to critical access areas (>500km in red). Interactive controls allow filtering by distance thresholds.

Critical Finding: 96 FSAs (18.8%) require >200km travel. 31 FSAs in extreme zones (>500km) face severe access barriers.

🎯 Map 2: Vulnerability Index

Composite multi-factor analysis combining geographic distance (50% weight), median household income (30%), and socioeconomic inequality (20%) to identify compound disadvantage zones.

Critical Finding: 27 FSAs face multiple barriers (>200km + income <$70k). 4 FSAs show extreme vulnerability scores (>70).

🏔️ Map 3: Indigenous Access Crisis

Targeted analysis highlighting severe disparities for communities with >5% Indigenous population. Color-coded overlays reveal systematic access inequities.

Critical Finding: Indigenous communities travel 1.93× farther (354.4km average vs 183.5km overall baseline).

⚠️ Map 4: Multi-Barrier Service Gaps

Advanced identification of FSAs experiencing multiple simultaneous barriers: distance, low income, inequality, Indigenous population, and visible minority status.

Critical Finding: 5 FSAs face 3+ simultaneous barriers. 104 FSAs experience compound disadvantage (2 barriers).

🔀 Map 5: Enhanced Patient Flow Analysis Enhanced

DRAMATICALLY ENHANCED! Animated patient flow visualization with directional arrows, 2.5× thicker lines, 60% opacity, and distance-based color coding. All 512 flows displayed with animation for maximum impact.

Enhancement: Directional arrows (FSA → Center), interactive hover tooltips, dramatic 5-tier color scheme, fully visible flows.

🔀 Interactive Sankey Flow Diagram New

Fully interactive Plotly Sankey visualization showing patient flows from provinces/regions to DBS centers. Hover for detailed statistics, explore relationships dynamically.

Insight: Visual representation of severe market concentration and cross-provincial patient flows revealing systemic imbalances.

📊 Vulnerability Matrix Analysis New

Static comprehensive matrix visualization displaying relationships between multiple vulnerability factors and geographic access barriers across all FSAs.

Analysis: Multi-dimensional vulnerability assessment enabling identification of compound risk patterns.

📈 Comprehensive Disparities Overview New

Publication-ready static overview presenting key disparity metrics, comparative statistics, and trend analyses across all measured dimensions in a single comprehensive view.

Comprehensive: All-in-one executive snapshot of major findings, disparities, and policy-relevant insights.

📥 Export & Download Options

All visualizations are available as standalone interactive HTML files. Static visualizations (PNG format) can be right-click saved for publications and presentations.
