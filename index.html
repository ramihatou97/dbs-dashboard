920 Patient Records • 511 Geographic Areas • 10 Treatment Centers • 13 Visualizations

Dataset Overview

Comprehensive analysis of geographic and socioeconomic barriers to DBS surgery access in Canada. Based on 920 verified patient records across 511 FSAs with data from Statistics Canada 2021 Census and Google Distance Matrix API. Interactive maps include distance metrics, household income, inequality indices, and demographic data.

Income Paradox

Distant patients have $16,000 lower median income than local patients ($78k vs $94k), revealing severe selection bias. Statistical validation: Spearman ρ=-0.1878 (p<0.001). Suggests only the most determined low-income patients overcome combined distance and cost barriers.

Atlantic Crisis

Complete regional access failure: 100% of Atlantic Canada DBS patients must travel >200km. Average distances: NL 2,924km, PEI 1,506km, NB 430km, NS 324km. Zero DBS centers serve 2.5 million Atlantic Canadians.

Research Summary

Analysis of 920 verified patient records across 511 geographic areas reveals significant access disparities. Key findings include income-distance paradox ($16k gap), regional access failures (Atlantic Canada: 100% >200km travel), and estimated unmet need of 340-1,360 patients based on distance decay modeling.
